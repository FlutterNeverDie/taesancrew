When you need workers, it's often unclear which way to go. Taesan Crew works in two ways: **job placement** and **subcontracting**.

## Job placement

- We introduce the number of people you need on the day you need them.
- You can adjust headcount to fit the site, by the day, by the week and so on.
- It works well for responding quickly when volume suddenly rises or someone is absent.

## Subcontracting

- You **hand over a whole unit of work**, such as packing, picking, inspection or inbound/outbound.
- Taesan Crew is responsible for staffing, site management and attendance records.
- It suits sites where it is hard to manage headcount every day.

## Not sure which one is right?

Just tell us the site location, the number of people needed, the type of work and the period. We will decide together on the approach that fits your situation.
