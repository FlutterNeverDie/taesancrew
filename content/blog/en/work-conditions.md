Here are the working conditions people ask about most before they start.

## Working conditions

- **Pay**: Same-day or next-day pay
- **Overtime pay**: Overtime is paid separately
- **Meals**: Free lunch
- **Commute**: Shuttle to work and back from the meeting point
- **Bonus**: Bonus if you have a Class 1 driver's license
- **Who can apply**: Beginners OK, foreigners OK (F-2, F-5, F-6; F-4 depends on the work), you can apply with a friend
- **Work days**: Working just one day or only weekends is fine

## The work

Most jobs are packing, picking, inspection, inbound/outbound and sorting at logistics centers. Many sites have no loading/unloading.

For details on sites and conditions, text or call any branch that suits you.
