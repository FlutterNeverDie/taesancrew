It's OK if this is your first logistics center job. At Taesan Crew, **you start work only after safety training**.

## What to text us

To apply, just send one text to any branch number. Please send the following.

1. **Name**
2. **Date of birth**
3. **Where you live**
4. **Preferred meeting point** (Gyeonggi-Gwangju / Seongnam / Cheonho)
5. **Start date**
6. **Preferred days** (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri / weekends only)

Numbers to text

- Gyeonggi-Gwangju Head Office **010-5801-2036**
- Seongnam Sujin Station Branch **010-2244-0031**
- Cheonho Branch **010-8141-0240**

## A typical day

1. Meet at your branch's meeting point (e.g. Sujin Station Exit 3, Cheonho Station Exit 6).
2. Ride together to the logistics center in our commute vehicle.
3. Do work such as packing, picking, inspection and inbound/outbound. Many sites have no loading/unloading.
4. After work, ride the vehicle back to the station.

## What to bring

- ID card
- Comfortable clothes and sneakers (safety shoes at some sites)

What you need can differ a little by site, so please ask the branch again when you apply.
