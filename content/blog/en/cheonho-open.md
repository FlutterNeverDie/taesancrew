Following the Gyeonggi-Gwangju Head Office and the Seongnam Sujin Station Branch, **Taesan Crew Cheonho Branch** is now open. You can now apply and ask questions at Cheonho Station too.

## Cheonho Branch

- **Address**: 서울 강동구 천호대로 1024, 힐스테이트 천호역 젠트리스 지하 2층 242호 (Hillstate Cheonho Station Gentris, B2 #242)
- **Getting here**: 1 min from Cheonho Station (천호역) Exit 6
- **Apply / questions**: 010-8141-0240

## Come from anywhere

No matter where you live, if Cheonho Station is easy for you to get to, contact the Cheonho Branch. You can apply right away with one text message.

For worker requests and subcontracting inquiries from companies, please call the head office main number **010-5801-2036** as before.
